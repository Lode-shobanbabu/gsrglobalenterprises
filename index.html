answered clearly

What sectors and procurement categories does GSR Global Enterprises specialize in?
GSR Global Enterprises delivers end-to-end turnkey procurement across five primary verticals: Heavy Commercial Fleet & APSRTC Automobile Spares, Biomedical Diagnostic & Hospital Equipment, Smart School & Digital Educational IT Infrastructure, Structural Steel & Industrial Metals, and Commercial Solar Clean Energy systems.
Is GSR Global Enterprises registered on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM)?
Yes. We are fully registered and compliant with a verified GSTIN (37ABDFG9557A1ZC), actively supporting GeM tender bidding, state government procurement guidelines, and formal public-sector institutional purchase orders across Andhra Pradesh.
What quality assurances and OEM warranties are provided with supplied components?
All mechanical spares, medical devices, and IT hardware are directly sourced from certified OEM manufacturers adhering to ISO 9001, ISO 13485 (medical), and CE standards. Every shipment includes original batch test certificates, manufacturer warranties, and dedicated SLA replacement support.
How does GSR Global Enterprises ensure on-time delivery across Andhra Pradesh?
We maintain dedicated multi-sector warehousing facilities and regional dispatch logistics across Andhra Pradesh, enabling fast order processing, verified transit insurance, and guaranteed door-step delivery to government depots, hospitals, and educational facilities within agreed SLA timeframes.
